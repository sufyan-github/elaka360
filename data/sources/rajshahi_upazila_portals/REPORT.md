# Rajshahi District upazila portals

- 30 pages on 9 portals (`raw/pages.csv`: URL, kind, update date). Mohanpur's host is `mohonpur.rajshahi.gov.bd`.
- **areas.csv (83):** 72 unions + 11 pourashavas, parsed from each upazila's union list.
- **facts.csv (156):** hand-transcribed from এক নজরে pages, with the printed text in the script (`F`); a value is dropped with a warning if its text disappears from the page.
- Bagha portal lists **7 unions** incl. চকরাজাপুর (chakrajapurup.rajshahi.gov.bd); Charghat's এক নজরে gives 2011 populations for its 6 unions and pourashava.

## Caveats

- Most এক নজরে pages date from 2013–2021 and quote the 2011 census.
- Internal inconsistencies recorded, not resolved: Tanore 212 mauzas / 210 villages vs 211 / 169 on its union page; Paba 339 km² (incl. pourashavas) vs 280.42; Bagha 185.16 vs 184.25 km².
- No Arani, Bhabaniganj or Naohata pourashava pages (the last two are unnamed templates). Kesharhat's printed area "১৬২১" is implausible and not recorded.
- Some pages claim a "2021 census" (there was none); flagged.
