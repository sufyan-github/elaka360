Saidpur Airport started its journey as a domestic airport in 1979. This airport
is situated on a total of 136.59 acres ofland. Saidpur Airport is managed by the
Civil Aviation Authority of Bangladesh.
This Airport is located at2 km from Saidpur town and 350km from
Dhaka, the capital city of Bangladesh.
The only destination is Dhaka for the Passengers. Following airlines are
presently operating their passenger flights from and to Saidpur Airport.
Biman Bangladesh Airlines
Novoair
US-Bangla Airlines.
