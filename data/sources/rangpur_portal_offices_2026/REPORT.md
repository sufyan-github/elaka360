## Gaps

- No structured addresses or coordinates.
- No pourashava list is published on rangpur.gov.bd; pourashavas appear only where an upazila menu links them.
- Union parishad rows have only a name, upazila and union number. The list links no UP portals, and "৬ নং কাফ্রিখাল" is listed twice (kept once).
- 4 office portals did not respond and are listed from the menu only. Some `www.*` sub-sites have self-signed certificates (e.g. www.badc.pirgonj.rangpur.gov.bd) or do not resolve.
- The Gaibandha DC phone is two numbers run together at source ("0258887750002588877502"), the same splitting issue as in the Rajshahi run.
- Several menu links on rangpurdiv.gov.bd are "#" (DGFI, NSI, education board, BTV and others) and are skipped.
