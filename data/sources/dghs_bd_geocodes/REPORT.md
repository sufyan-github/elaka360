# DGHS bd-geocodes (BBS geocodes) — Rajshahi Division

- **Source:** DGHS Bangladesh Core FHIR Implementation Guide, CodeSystem `bd-geocodes` v0.5.0 (2026-10-15), 1,591 codes nationally. https://fhir.dghs.gov.bd/core/CodeSystem-bd-geocodes.html
- **Licence:** CC0-1.0 (the guide's licence field).
- **Why this source:** bbs.gov.bd's geocode page was unreachable (TLS error / redirect to home page).
- **Code layout (inferred):** 2 digits division (50) · 4 district (5081) · 6 city corporation (508166) · 8 upazila or city thana (50810010) · 10 paurashava (5081001008 Arani).
- **Coverage:** 8 districts, 68 upazila codes (Naldanga appears twice: 50690055 and 50690066), Rajshahi City Corporation with 6 thanas (Boalia, Chandrima, Kashiadanga, Matihar, Rajpara, Shah Makhdum), 61 paurashavas (Rajshahi District 14). **No unions.**
- **Agreement:** the 8-digit upazila codes equal the HDX v03 P-codes minus `BD` for all 67 upazilas.
- **Crosswalk:** districts 8/8, upazilas 67/67, Rajshahi District paurashavas 14/14. Bogura City Corporation is absent (DGHS lists "Bogra Paurasava").
- **Spelling differences from HDX:** Chapainawabganj/Chapainababganj, Dhupchanchia/Dupchachia, Sonatola/Sonatala, Baghmara/Bagmara, Chauhali/Chouhali, Royganj/Rayganj, Ullahpara/Ullapara.
- **Code vintage:** not stated by DGHS.
