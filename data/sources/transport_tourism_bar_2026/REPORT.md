## How rows were made

- **BIWTA river ports** (`transport`, proposed `transport.river_port`): the 54 gazetted inland river ports from the PDF linked on BIWTA's 'বন্দর ও পরিবহন' page. The PDF is typed in Bijoy (SutonnyMJ); its text layer is converted to Unicode. Rows 48–54, row 32, row 22's name and a few district cells use a font whose text layer is broken; those values were read from the rendered page and are applied only where the converted text has the known broken form (noted on the row). A port spanning several districts is filed under the first district listed (all in `extra_json`). Gazette notification numbers and dates are kept. River ports are port-limit areas, not single ghats.
- **Speedboat routes** (`facts.csv`, and `extra_json.speedboat_routes` on the river-port row): Ministry of Shipping notice of 24-09-2026 (linked on BIWTA's ভাড়াসূচি page). The PDF's text layer drops conjuncts, so the 44 route names were read from the rendered page; `fetch.py` checks that the fares in the PDF still match the transcription in order and stops otherwise. `district_en` of a route fact is the district of the BIWTA river port it is listed under.
- **Bus terminals**: Dhaka South City Corporation's and Mymensingh City Corporation's 'বাস টার্মিনাল' pages (names only; Mymensingh's embedded map centres kept in `extra_json.map_embed_center`, not used as coordinates).
- **Parjatan units**: every unit page linked under 'অধীনস্থ হোটেল/মোটেল' on parjatan.gov.bd: name, address, the unit's landlines / corporate mobiles and mailbox as printed (no staff names), booking link. Hotels/motels/holiday complexes use the top-level `business` key with proposed `business.hotel`; restaurants `business.restaurant`; tourist centres/picnic spots `tourism`.
- **Star-rated hotels**: Ministry of Civil Aviation and Tourism (Hotel & Restaurant Cell) five/four/three-star lists: name, address, star rating; district from the address. `business` (proposed `business.hotel`).
- **Parjatan attractions**: the corporation's district pages (division page → district page). Only the attraction headings are taken (numbered, or on some pages a short line ending in a colon); the section heading (historical/archaeological, religious, natural, cultural …) sets the category, refined by words in the name (museum, beach, waterfall, tea garden, park, mosque/temple …). Descriptions are not copied. Festivals, foods, crafts, boat trips, peoples and one-word generic entries listed as 'tourism products' are left out (89 nationally).
- **Tourism Board destinations**: beautifulbangladesh.gov.bd division and category list pages (plus any destination in the site's sitemap that no list page shows): English name, district (URL), location label, and the map point of the card's Google Maps link. A point outside the row's district is dropped (kept in `extra_json.published_latlon`); where the point and the location label agree on another district than the URL, that district is used (noted). 'tasty-food' entries (dishes) are left out; 'luxury' hotels use `business` (proposed `business.hotel`).
- **Forest Department protected areas**: the 58-row 'রক্ষিত এলাকার তালিকা' (national parks, wildlife and dolphin sanctuaries, eco-parks, special biodiversity areas, marine protected areas, botanical garden) plus the other eco-park and kunjaban pages (deduplicated by name); area (ha) and notification kept. National parks, eco-parks, gardens → `tourism.park`; sanctuaries and other areas → `tourism.natural`. Marine protected areas in the Bay of Bengal and Purbachal's area (no district) are not placed.
- District/upazila: normalised to the seed's English names; the published value is kept in `extra_json` where it differs (`districts_published`, `location_published`, `location_label`).

## Data-quality notes

- The river-port list is as of 02-06-2025 (last notification); BIWTA's page was checked on 2026-11-19.
- Parjatan unit phones: landlines and the corporation's own 01991-139xxx mobile series are kept; other mobile numbers on the unit pages (possibly managers' phones) are not collected (`extra_json.mobiles_not_collected` counts them). The units' own mailboxes (some on gmail) are kept as printed.
- Tourism Board map points are Google Maps links chosen by the site editors, not surveyed positions.
- Parjatan attraction pages mix tourist sites with institutions (universities, colleges) listed as attractions; they are kept as published under `tourism` and may duplicate rows from UGC/BANBEIS/DoA sources.
