# Bangladesh Post postcode booklet — Rajshahi Division

- **Source:** bdpost.gov.bd publishes no office list (its postcode menu points to ekdak.com). The only official list is a scanned, image-only postcode booklet on post.rajshahi.gov.bd (undated; page updated 2018). Pages 21–23 were transcribed by hand from the page images in `raw/`; `fetch.py` checks the PDF's sha256 and writes the transcription. Licence: none stated.

## Counts: 138 offices (government.post_office)

Rajshahi 25 (GPO 6000; Ghoramara HO 6100; 7 town sub-offices 6201–6207; 9 upazila post offices and their sub-offices), Sirajganj 23, Bogura 20, Naogaon 19, Pabna 18, Natore 14, Chapainawabganj 12, Joypurhat 7. `extra_json`: postcode, office type (GPO/HO/UPO/TSO/SO/EDSO), booklet page. `facts.csv`: 66 rows.

## Gaps

Branch offices are not in the booklet; the 88-page all-offices list and the telephone directory are image-only and not transcribed; old spellings (সিংরা, পুটিয়া); Chauhali's head office listed under Tangail; no coordinates or contacts. University of Rajshahi post office: 6205.
