## Caveats

- এক নজরে carries both **72** (headline table) and a stale **71** (সমাজসেবা section, next to 639 wards = 71 × 9).
- The pourashava list swaps the parents of মুন্ডুমালা (listed under Godagari) and কাকনহাট (listed under Tanore). Upazila portals and BBS 2022 say Kakanhat → Godagari, Mundumala → Tanore. DOUBT notes on those rows.
- Thanas "১৩ (৪ metropolitan)" is stale against RMP's 12.
- Population 3,023,216 is a March 2020 projection; mauzas 1,718 vs BBS 1,678.
- No English names printed. No licence stated (facts only).
