# Rangpur City Corporation (rpcc.gov.bd)

- **Portal:** the official portal is **rpcc.gov.bd**, linked from rangpurdiv.gov.bd and rangpur.gov.bd; rpcc.portal.gov.bd does not resolve. Key RANGPUR_CITY_CORPORATION, accessed 2026-11-17.
- **Wards: 33**, from "এক নজরে" (updated 2025-04-28) and the ward-delimitation gazette (Bangladesh Gazette extraordinary, 9 Sep 2012, attached as a scanned PDF).
  - areas.csv has the city corporation and wards 1–33, by number only; RPCC publishes no ward names.
  - The gazette groups them into **11 reserved (women's) wards** of 3 consecutive wards each. This was read from the scan, not machine-checked.
  - The gazette's ward→mouza lists were not transcribed.
  - RpMP's history page says "30 wards", which is outdated.
- **Established:**
  - City corporation: 28/06/2012 (RpMP's page says 1 July 2012).
  - Pourashava since 1 May 1869; class A since 23/09/1986.
- **Area:** 205.70 km² ("এক নজরে") vs 203.63 km² ("অবস্থান ও আয়তন", 2022). Both are kept and the conflict is noted.
- **Population:** about 796,556 (male 398,282, female 398,274), literacy 65%. The page says "প্রায়" and gives no year or basis; it is not the BBS 2022 figure.
- **Mouzas and mahallas:** 112 mouzas and 442 mahallas (no names published).
- **Merged unions:** 12 unions were merged in (10 of Rangpur Sadar, Sarai of Kaunia, Kalyani of Pirgachha), 7 whole and 5 partial. The cantonment is excluded.
- **Not copied:** the councillor and ward lists are scanned images or PDFs with names and phones.
- There is no thana list on the RPCC portal (see RANGPUR_METRO_POLICE).
- Licence: none stated.
