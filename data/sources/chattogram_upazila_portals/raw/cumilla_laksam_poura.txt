Location of Laksam municipality in the middle of Laksam upazila. It is bounded on the west by Kandirpar Union, on the southwest by Gobindpur Union, on the south by Uttarada Union and Azgara Union, on the east by Laksam East Union and Perul South Union of Lalmai Upazila and on the north by Perul South Union of Lalmai Upazila.
Laksam municipality has 9 wards. Administrative activities of this municipality are under Laksam police station. It is part of Comilla-9, the 256th constituency of the Jatiya Sangsad.
