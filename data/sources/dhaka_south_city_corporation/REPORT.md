# Dhaka South City Corporation (dscc.gov.bd)

- **Portal:** dscc.gov.bd (www.dscc.gov.bd fails TLS verification). Key DHAKA_SOUTH_CITY_CORPORATION, `"city": 50002`, accessed 2026-11-17. Collected by `rcc/fetch.py --division dhaka --city dhaka_south`.
- **Wards: 75** ("এক নজরে", updated 2026-02-24): 75 general-ward and 25 reserved-ward councillors. History: 57 wards at the split, plus 18 wards formed from 8 fringe unions after the NICAR decision of 9 May 2016. areas.csv has wards 1–75 by number (name_bn is a label).
- **Zones: 10.** The zone-office table ("জরুরী যোগাযোগ", updated 2024-09-16) gives the wards of each zone; ward 42 is listed under zones 4 and 5 and ward 44 under none (possibly a typing error, not corrected; ward 44 has no zone in areas.csv). The ward-location page is a scanned 2021 PDF (zone → ward → mahallas, "per the gazette"), not transcribed.
- **Area:** 109.24 km² (the seed has 109.25).
- **Population:**
  - 2011 census: 3,883,423 (both pages).
  - 2022 census: **conflict** — "এক নজরে" prints "৪৩,০৪৮৯৩" (4,304,893), the history page "৪২,৯৯, ৩৪৫" (4,299,345, the seed's figure). Both are kept.
  - Voters 2,367,488 (2020).
- **Dates:** Dhaka Pourashava "স্থাপিত" 1 Aug 1864; DCC dissolved 23 Nov 2011 and divided from 4 Dec 2011; DNCC and DSCC started 1 Feb 2012.
- **Thanas:** "থানার সংখ্যা ২৩টি", but DSCC does not name them.
- **Not copied:** administrator's and architects' names, zone officers, ward secretaries.
- Licence: none stated.
