# Rajshahi City Corporation (erajshahi.portal.gov.bd)

- The official portal is **erajshahi.portal.gov.bd**; rcc.gov.bd and rcc.portal.gov.bd do not resolve.
- **Wards: 30** (মহানগরী দর্পণ, 2026-03-04; এক নজরে, 2024-10-14). Ward list is numbers 01–30 only; the ward PDFs contain personal names and phones, which were **not** copied. No ward names or mahalla lists are published (134 mahallas in total).
- **Area:** 96.72 km² (RDA master plan; 48.06 km² land, the rest river).
- **History:** municipality 1869; municipal corporation 1987-08-13 (page prints "১৮৮৭", a typo); city corporation 1990.
- **Population:** 553,288 (2022 census, same as BBS C-01). The seed's 552,791 is wrong. 2011: 449,756.
- **Thanas (RCC):** কাশিয়াডাঙ্গা, রাজপাড়া, বোয়ালিয়া, শাহ্ মখদুম, চন্দ্রিমা, মতিহার (BBS 2022 gives the same 6 with 16/7/4/3/2/2 wards).
