## How rows were placed

- District: the list's own district field where it has one (spelling normalised to `data/seed/geo/admin_areas.csv`; the published value is kept as `district_published`), else the last district named in the address, else the published coordinates (point in `districts.geojson`); then, only if none of these names a district: a seed upazila named in the address whose name is unique in the country, a postcode (Bangladesh Post list, `bdpost_postcodes_*` sources), a well-known metropolitan locality (Motijheel, Agrabad …), or the controlling branch's district. ASA publishes coordinates and village names only, so ASA rows are placed by their coordinates first (its own district-office unit is the fallback). `extra_json.district_from` says which rule placed the row (`field`, `text`, `point`, `upazila`, `postcode`, `locality`, `parent`). Rows with no district are left out (counted above as 'without a district').
- Upazila: the list's upazila/thana field matched within the district, else a seed upazila named in the address. Metropolitan areas (e.g. Gulshan, Kotwali) are not upazilas and stay empty.
- Coordinates: only as published; swapped lat/lon corrected; points outside Bangladesh or outside the row's district are dropped (kept in `extra_json.published_latlon`, with a note).
- Names: the organisation's short name is prefixed when the published name lacks it; the published name is kept in `extra_json.published_name`. Branch code, routing number, SWIFT, outlet type, controlling branch, opening dates are in `extra_json` where published.

## Categories

`finance.bank` branches, sub-branches, AD branches, agent-banking outlets (outlet type in `extra_json.outlet_kind`); `finance.atm` ATMs, CRMs, CDMs, ADMs and booths; `business.service` operators' care centres / service points and courier branches / hubs; `health.diagnostic_center`, `health.hospital`, `health.pharmacy`; `business.supermarket`; `other.ngo` NGO / microfinance offices.
Proposed taxonomy keys (not used until added): `finance.agent_banking` (agent-banking outlets), `business.courier` (courier / parcel branches and hubs), `business.telecom_service` (mobile-operator care centres), `finance.microfinance` (NGO-MFI branch offices).

## Data-quality notes

- Lists are as current as each website (Nov 2026); several are old (Dhaka Bank ATM/ADM rows last edited 2015–17; DBBL sub-branch addresses are often just a building). Closed rows are skipped where the site marks them (UCB, AB Bank).
- Some sources mix kinds (MTB's branch feed includes sub-branches; IBBL's ATM list flags CRMs).
- A district field can be wrong (BRAC Bank, IFIC ATMs, UCB `data-district`): for those sources the address is preferred and conflicts are noted on the row.
- Phone numbers are the branch / office numbers as listed (many organisations publish branch mobiles).
- Bangladesh Krishi Bank has no branches in Rajshahi and Rangpur divisions (Rajshahi Krishi Unnayan Bank serves them).
- `raw/` is not committed: the national lists are large (DBBL's ATM page ~6 MB, MTB's page ~60 MB, Popular's JS bundle ~16 MB); `fetch.py` rebuilds everything (about 1 h, mostly Rupali's ~590 branch-detail pages and ASA's ~780 office lookups at 1.2 s per request). `--all-divisions` fetches each national list once and writes all eight folders.
- Grameenphone: while scouting, about 64 store-locator requests were made before its terms of use (which forbid automated collection) were found; that data was deleted and nothing from it is used.
