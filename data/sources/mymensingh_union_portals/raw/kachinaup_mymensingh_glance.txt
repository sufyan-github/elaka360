Kachina Union at a glance
One of the traditional areas of Bhaluka Upazila bearing the witness of Kaler is Kachina Union to the east. Most of the area of ​​this union is flat. In the course of time, Kachina Union still has its own identity in various fields including education, culture, religious ceremonies and sports.
A) Name - 9th Kachina Union Parishad.
B) Area - 584 (acres)
C) Population - 25612 people (approximately) (according to 2011 census)
D) Number of villages - 6
E) Number of mouzas - 5
F) Number of Hats / Markets-6.
G) Means of communication from Upazila Sadar - CNG / Rickshaw.
