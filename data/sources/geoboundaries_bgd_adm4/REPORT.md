# geoBoundaries BGD ADM4 — Rajshahi Division

- **Source:** geoBoundaries gbOpen BGD-ADM4-29066614, year represented 2020, built 2023-12-12, derived from the HDX "administrative-boundaries-of-bangladesh-as-of-2015" dataset (now 404).
- **Licence:** CC BY 3.0 IGO. Attribution: geoBoundaries (Runfola et al. 2020); BBS; OCHA ROAP.
- **Coverage:** 654 polygons: 564 unions, 60 paurashavas, 30 RCC wards. Rajshahi District: 71 unions, 14 paurashavas, 30 wards.
- **Codes:** HDX 2020 P-codes attached by point-in-polygon; 654/654 matched one-to-one with identical names. Use `../hdx_cod_ab_bgd/crosswalk.csv`.
- **Coordinates:** lat/lon is the area-weighted centroid; 16 centroids fall outside their polygon and are flagged.
- **Area:** `area_km2` computed by Elaka360, not official.
- **Vintage:** ~2015–2020; Naldanga's unions are under Natore Sadar. No Bangla names.
- **Not committed:** the ~340 MB national file; `fetch.py` rebuilds from it.
