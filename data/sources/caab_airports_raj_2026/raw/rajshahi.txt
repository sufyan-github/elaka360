Shah Makhdum Airport is a Domestic Airport at Situated at the Western side of
the country. It is about 10 km North of the Rajshahi city and beside
Rajshahi-Nowgon highway. Now two Passenger flights ( Biman Bangladesh Airlines
and US-Bangla Airlines and Two Aviation academy) are operating in this Airport
.Another airlines Novo Air will operate very soon. Two Aviation Academy (Pilot
training school) are running here . They have six/ seven training Aircraft those
are operating from this Airport.
Besides this domestic cargo flights are also operating to and from different
destination at Shah Makhdum Airport.
