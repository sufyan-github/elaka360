## How rows were placed

- District: the list's own district field (spelling normalised to `data/seed/geo/admin_areas.csv`; published value kept as `district_published`), else the district of the office's national-portal host (`<office>.<upazila>.<district>.gov.bd`), else the last district named in the name / address; then, only if none names a district: an upazila of the parent organisation's district named in the office name (PBS zonal offices), a seed upazila whose name is unique in the country, a well-known metropolitan locality, or the parent office (Titas branches). LGED's upazila list gives English upazila names only: names found in several districts are placed by their neighbours in the district-ordered list (`district_from: sequence`, with a note). `extra_json.district_from` says which rule placed each row. Rows with no district are left out (counted above).
- Upazila: the list's upazila field or the portal host's upazila label, matched within the district; else a seed upazila named in the office name / address. Division, region and district offices get no upazila.
- Names: as published; the organisation's short name is prefixed when the published name lacks it (published name kept in `extra_json.published_name` / `published_name_en`). DESCO's sales-and-distribution rows are 'বিক্রয় ও বিতরণ বিভাগ, <name>' (the table's column heading + the name). DSS rows are named from the list's title and the upazila in the office's official e-mail (see `upazila_from_email`).

## Categories

Existing keys only: `government` (offices), `emergency.utility_emergency` (DESCO customer-service centres, Titas emergency units), `emergency.water_sanitation` (WASA maintenance-and-operation zones), `agriculture` (fisheries offices). Proposed sub-keys, in `extra_json.proposed_category`: `government.utility_office` (electricity / gas / water offices), `government.engineering_office` (LGED), `government.social_services_office` (DSS), `government.cooperative_office`, `agriculture.fisheries_office`.

## Privacy filters

Not collected: officers' names and designations (DESCO, DPDC, Titas, WASA and NESCO tables, PBS zonal pages), officers' and post-assigned mobiles (REB's GM mobiles, LGED, DSS, WZPDCO, DPDC, Titas, fisheries 'corporate SIM'), and e-mails that are not on *.gov.bd or the organisation's own domain (e.g. PBS gmail addresses). Mobiles are kept only where the organisation publishes them as an office's hotline / complaint-centre / customer-service number (PBS hotlines, NESCO complaint centres, DESCO customer-service centres).

## Data-quality notes

- Lists are as current as each website (Nov 2026); some pages were last edited years ago (DSS 2017, fisheries 2021, REB's hotline list 2022). LGED's mobile/e-mail list is dated March 2024.
- DWASA's zone pages give the areas a zone covers (kept as `areas_covered`), not the zone office's address.
- PBS zonal / sub-zonal offices are named after a bazaar, union or upazila; those whose name or address does not identify a seed district (about a quarter, e.g. 'চন্দ্রগঞ্জ জোনাল অফিস') are left out rather than given the PBS's district. A PBS head office's own address is not on REB's lists, so head offices have a district (from the PBS name / website) but no address or upazila. pbs1.sylhet.gov.bd and pbs2.sylhet.gov.bd failed TLS verification (not fetched without verification): their head offices are listed from REB's pages only.
- Titas lists the same branch name under a sales and a revenue division; the division's abbreviation is added to the branch name (`branch_name` keeps the published one). Branch / zonal-office rows have no address.
- `raw/` is not committed: the pages carry officers' names and mobiles. `fetch.py` rebuilds everything (≈ 15 min; the 80 PBS sites are read one after another at ≥ 1.2 s per host).
