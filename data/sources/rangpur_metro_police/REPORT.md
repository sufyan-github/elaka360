# Rangpur Metropolitan Police (rpmp.gov.bd)

- **Site:** the official site is **rpmp.gov.bd**, RpMP's own CMS rather than the national portal framework; its pages carry no update date. The divisional portal also links rpmp.rangpurdiv.gov.bd, which is thin and used only for the HQ address "কাচারী বাজার, রংপুর-৫৪০০". Key RANGPUR_METRO_POLICE, accessed 2026-11-17.
- **6 thanas in 3 zones** (English names are transliterations):
  - Kotwali zone: কোতয়ালী (at মূলাটোল), তাজহাট
  - Mahiganj zone: মাহিগঞ্জ, হারাগাছ
  - Parshuram zone: পরশুরাম, হাজিরহাট
- **Coverage per thana:** each thana page lists the RPCC wards and mouzas (with JL numbers) it covers. These go into the facts `rpcc_wards_covered`, `mouza_count` and `mouzas`.

  - Mouza spellings are as printed; JL numbers are per upazila.
- **History:** organogram gazette 10 Dec 2017 (1,185 posts, 123 vehicles); রংপুর মহানগরী পুলিশ আইন ২০১৮ passed 12 Apr 2018; inaugurated 16 Sep 2018.
- **No area or population:** RpMP publishes neither. Its history page's "205 km², ~10 lakh, 30 wards" describes the city corporation and conflicts with RPCC.
