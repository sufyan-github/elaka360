Sylhet Metropolitan Police (SMP) was formed on 26 August 2006
The total area of SMP is about 518.43 Sq.km including 27 wards of Sylhet City Corporation, 08 Unions of Sylhet Sadar Upazila and 10 Unions of South Surma Upazilla under Sylhet district.
SMP area is divided into six Police stations as Kotwali Model Thana, Jalalabad Thana, Airport Thana, South Surma Thana, Moglabazar Thana and Shahporan Thana.
The new AOR getting all administrative approval of the government was published on 10th May 2011
On 11August 2011, SMP starts its full strength operation
