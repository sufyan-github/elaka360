# DGHS bd-geocodes (BBS geocodes) — Dhaka Division

- **Source:** DGHS Bangladesh Core FHIR Implementation Guide, CodeSystem `bd-geocodes` v0.5.0 (2026-10-15), 1,591 codes nationally. https://fhir.dghs.gov.bd/core/CodeSystem-bd-geocodes.html
- **Run:** `python3 -I data/sources/dghs_bd_geocodes/fetch.py --division dhaka` (accessed 2026-11-17). Key `DGHS_BD_GEOCODES_DHAKA`, division `dhaka`, kind `dghs_geocodes`.
- **Licence:** CC0-1.0 (the guide's licence field).
- **Code layout (inferred, as for Rajshahi):** 2 digits division (30) · 4 district (3026) · 6 city corporation (302620 Dhaka South, 302625 Dhaka North, 303333 Gazipur, 306744 Narayanganj) · 8 upazila or city thana · 10 paurashava.
- **Coverage:** 13 districts, 4 city corporations, 92 upazila codes (89 real + 3 doubtful, below), 58 city thanas (Dhaka South 24, Dhaka North 26, Gazipur 8; none for Narayanganj City Corporation), 63 paurashavas. **No unions:** the 102 "Unions Of … Upazila" placeholders are skipped.
- **Dhaka North/South thanas with union groups.** 13 thana codes (Demra, Jatrabari, Kadamtali, Kamrangir Char, Khilgaon, Mugda Para, Sabujbag in Dhaka South; Badda, Dakshinkhan, Bhatara, Khilkhet, Turag, Uttar Khan in Dhaka North) also have "Unions Of X Upazila". These are the unions absorbed in 2016. `fetch.py` now reads these 13 codes as metro thanas, not upazilas (table `METRO_CC_WITH_UNION_GROUPS`), and says so in `notes`. Gazipur Sadar (30333330), Bandar (30674406) and Narayanganj Sadar (30674458) lie in city-corporation code space too but are real upazilas, and are read as upazilas, as Rangpur Sadar was.
- **Doubtful codes, kept as printed and flagged in `notes`:** Gaffargaon `30260022` (with Gaffargaon Paurashava `3026002222`), Gauripur `30260023` and Sreebardi `30260090` (with Sreebardi Paurashava `3026009077`) are listed under Dhaka district. The same upazila numbers are used for Gaffargaon/Gauripur in Mymensingh (4561…22/23 "Unions Of …") and Sreebardi in Sherpur (45890090), so these are probably misfiled from the pre-2015 Dhaka Division. They are unmatched in the crosswalk.
- **Spelling in source:** "Dhaka North City Corperation". It is matched to seed 50001 by a hand-checked code table (`CC_SEED`, method `code_city_corporation`).
- **Agreement with HDX v03:** the 8-digit upazila codes equal the HDX v03 P-codes minus `BD` for 86 of 89 upazilas. Exceptions: Gazipur Sadar `30333330` vs HDX `BD30330030`, Bandar `30674406` vs `BD30670006`, Narayanganj Sadar `30674458` vs `BD30670058`. City-corporation codes are 6 digits here vs 8 in HDX (e.g. `302625` vs `BD30262500`).
- **Crosswalk:** districts 13/13; city corporations 4/4 (50001, 50002, 50010, 50011); upazilas 88/89 (77 exact, 2 banglish, 8 skeleton, 1 fuzzy: Zanjira↔Zajira). Lohajang↔Louhajanj is unmatched (similarity 0.75). Paurashavas 0/63: the seed has no Dhaka pourashavas.
- **Paurashavas (63):** Dhaka 5 (Dhamrai, Dohar, Savar + doubtful Gaffargaon, Sreebardi); Faridpur 5 (Bhanga, Boalmari, Faridpur, Madhukhali, Nagarkanda); Gazipur 3 (Kaliakair, Kaliganj, Sreepur); Gopalganj 4; Kishoreganj 8; Madaripur 4 (Kalkini, Madaripur, Rajoir, Shibchar); Manikganj 2; Munshiganj 2 (Mirkadim, Munshiganj); Narayanganj 5 (Araihazar, Gopaldi, Sonargaon, Kanchan, Tarabo); Narsingdi 6; Rajbari 3; Shariatpur 5; Tangail 11 (incl. Basail, Elenga).
- **Sets disagree between sources:** BBS 2022 has 63 paurashavas (Faridpur 6, Shariatpur 6), HDX 2020 points 59.
- **Code vintage:** not stated by DGHS.
