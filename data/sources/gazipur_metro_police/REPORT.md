# Gazipur Metropolitan Police (gmp.gov.bd)

- **Site:** gmp.gov.bd (GMP's own PHP site, no update dates; no robots.txt). Key GAZIPUR_METRO_POLICE, `"city": 50011`, accessed 2026-11-17. Collected by `rmp/fetch.py --division dhaka --city gazipur`.
- **8 thanas in 4 zones** ("About GMP"):
  - Crime North: Sadar zone (গাজীপুর সদর, বাসন), Konabari zone (কোনাবাড়ী, কাশিমপুর);
  - Crime South: Gacha zone (গাছা, পূবাইল), Tongi zone (টঙ্গী পূর্ব, টঙ্গী পশ্চিম).
  - English names as on the GMP menu ("Sadar Thana" → Gazipur Sadar).
- **City corporation (area_en): 8 of 8 = Gazipur City Corporation.** GMP states that the metropolitan area is made up of the 57 wards of Gazipur City Corporation; recorded in each thana's notes (areas.csv has no `area_en` column) and as the fact `jurisdiction`.
- **facts:** started 16 Sep 2018 (Gazipur Metropolitan Police Ordinance 2018); about 330 km²; about 65 lakh people (`estimate`; GCC says about 4 million); 8 thanas, 4 zones.
- No ward or mouza coverage per thana is published. Phones are in GAZIPUR_METRO_POLICE_THANAS_2026.
